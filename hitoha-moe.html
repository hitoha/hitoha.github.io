What it is

The site opened in December 2019 as HITOHA Labs: one place for news, downloads and write-ups for projects that had been scattered around, starting with the Internet Friendly Media Encoder.

Since then it has grown into a long-running notebook. Most posts are practical: MikroTik and IPv6 setups, DNS, GPON and fibre tinkering, Linux and homelab guides, A.I. experiments and software releases. Anime, games, travel and the occasional Malay–Japanese Eurobeat track fill the gaps.
