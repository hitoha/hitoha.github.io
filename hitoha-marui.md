## The quiet triplet

Hitoha is one of the three sixth-graders at the centre of Norio Sakurai's gag manga
*Mitsudomoe*. Her sisters fill every room they enter: Mitsuba is loud and bossy, Futaba
is all muscle and energy. Hitoha sits at her desk in class 6-3 with a flat expression
and her eyes on a book, and lets the chaos happen around her.

## What she's like

- **Always reading.** The book goes everywhere with her, wrapped in a plain cover, and
  she would much rather nobody looked inside.
- **A dark aura.** Her silence and her stare make classmates nervous. Most of the time
  she is simply shy and has no idea how to start a conversation.
- **Soft underneath.** She dotes on Chikubi, the class hamster, and regularly camps
  under her homeroom teacher's desk to be near him.
- **A secret hero fan.** She loves the tokusatsu show *Gachi Rangers*, a passion she
  shares with that same teacher, Satoshi Yabe. Their friendship is one of the series'
  longest-running sources of misunderstanding.

## One leaf

The triplets' names count down. *Mitsuba* reads as "three leaves", *Futaba* as "two
leaves", and *Hitoha* as "one leaf": the eldest gets three and the youngest gets one.
The title *Mitsudomoe* is the three-comma crest, and by extension a three-way struggle,
which is a fair description of life in the Marui house.

## About Mitsudomoe

*Mitsudomoe* began in Akita Shoten's *Weekly Shōnen Champion* in 2006, later moved to
*Bessatsu Shōnen Champion*, and finished in 2017 with 19 collected volumes. It follows
the triplets, their classmates and their hapless new teacher through one long string of
misheard words and wrong conclusions.

Studio Bridge adapted it for television: 13 episodes in the summer of 2010, followed by
the 8-episode *Mitsudomoe Zōryōchū!* in early 2011. Hitoha is voiced by Haruka Tomatsu.
